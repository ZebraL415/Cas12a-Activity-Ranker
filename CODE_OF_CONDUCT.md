# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and scientifically honest collaboration environment.

Project participants are expected to:

- communicate constructively and focus criticism on ideas and evidence;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels and disciplines;
- disclose uncertainty, protocol changes and negative results;
- protect private biological data and contributor information;
- avoid harassment, discriminatory language and personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation after repeated or serious violations. Sensitive reports should be sent through the repository's private security-reporting channel rather than a public issue.
